# Data Card: DP-Fair Synthetic EHR (Stage 3 release candidate)

*Structure follows Datasheets for Datasets (Gebru et al., 2021). Compiled
2026-07-12 from the generation manifests rather than entered by hand.*

## Motivation

Produced to test whether synthetic EHR can support ICU predictive modelling without
exposing patients. It is the compliance-vetted dataset generation of a four-pillar
research programme, created by the author, Belhassen Ouerghi.

## Composition

* **Instances.** Synthetic ICU-stay feature rows, and one synthetic daily
  ICU-admission count series. Counts: 79,693 rows for the length-of-stay task,
  69,699 for the readmission task, plus the count series.
* **Each instance.** De-identified clinical features (physiology, demographics,
  utilisation) matching the schema of the three replication tasks. No identifiers,
  no free text, no dates.
* **Sensitive attributes present**, by design, so that fairness can be audited:
  gender; insurance (readmission), gender (LOS).
* **Synthetic, not real.** Every row is generated, and no instance corresponds to a
  real patient. Membership-inference attack AUC is at chance; see the model card.
* **Label source.** Generated jointly with the features by Fair-DP-CTGAN (fair conditional generation on a DP-SGD critic; RDP-accounted).

## Collection process

Not collected from subjects. Derived by training a differentially private generator
on de-identified MIMIC-IV v3.1 cohorts under PhysioNet credentialed access.
Generation parameters: 100 epochs, a 30,000-row fitting
subsample, a noise multiplier of 1.01, and the `fairsample` arm.

## Preprocessing, cleaning and labelling

Continuous columns are mode-specific-normalised with a variational Gaussian mixture,
and discrete columns are one-hot encoded. Numeric missingness is median-imputed
before fitting, which is a documented non-private residual, recorded as RR-2 in the
risk register. Fair conditional sampling rebalances subgroup shares at generation
time, at no additional privacy cost.

## Uses

* **Suitable for.** Methodological research on synthetic-data utility, privacy and
  fairness; benchmarking under the train-on-synthetic, test-on-real protocol;
  teaching.
* **Not suitable for.** Training or validating any model intended for clinical care;
  any inference about real individuals; any use that implies the data are real.
  Utility is materially below that of models trained on real data, as the model card
  records.
* **Representation caveat.** Subgroup shares are rebalanced toward a uniform target
  rather than matched to the population, and under differential privacy they vary
  substantially between training runs. Medicare share: 0.542 in the
  real cohort against 0.368 in this release (0.350 ± 0.184 across
  5 seeds of the same recipe).

## Distribution and maintenance

Held within the credentialed research environment and not externally released,
pending the human gates recorded in the impact assessment. Provenance and byte
integrity are recorded in the release manifest and its checksums. The dataset is
regenerated by the pipeline and will be superseded by the realism-stage generation.
